! Erasing will unpair Bluetooth

Erase wipes the board's stored Bluetooth bond. If this device was paired with your phone (the All Cameras Are Beacons app), you'll need to remove the existing pairing in your phone's Bluetooth settings and pair again, otherwise the app won't reconnect. A normal flash without erase keeps the pairing.
ACAB
All Cameras Are Beacons

Counter-surveillance detection firmware for the Colonel Panic OUI-Spy and Mesh-Detect boards (XIAO ESP32-S3). Plug the board into USB, pick a build, and flash it from this page. No toolchain is required. The beacon board is not flashed from here; see below.

ACAB is passive detect-and-report only. It listens for the advertisements these devices already broadcast, with no jamming, spoofing, or interference.
! Web flashing needs the Web Serial API, so use Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop. Safari and Firefox do not work. The board must be connected by USB-C.
! Own a beacon board? Don't flash it from this page. These are the public XIAO builds. On a beacon board they drop the second radio's detections and put the buzzer on the wrong pin, so alerts go silent, and on rev-B hardware they leave out the power gate entirely, so hold-to-off and the app's power-off both stop working: on battery there is then no way to switch the unit off, and it runs until the cell is flat. USB recovery is the only way back. Use the board's own flasher instead: rev-A beacon flasher or rev-B beacon flasher. Those two are not interchangeable either. Normally you don't need either one: the app delivers beacon updates over Bluetooth.

OUI-Spy APP

An app-controlled scanner that streams detections to the All Cameras Are Beacons phone apps (iPhone and Android) over BLE and beeps a distinct tone for each target class. Runs on the Colonel Panic OUI-Spy (XIAO ESP32-S3).

  • BLE + WiFi scanning
  • ACAB GATT service
  • buzzer / LED alerts

The board pairs with the All Cameras Are Beacons iPhone and Android apps for live detections and controls. Get the iPhone beta free on TestFlight via the README.

Browser not supported. Use Chrome or Edge. Must be served over HTTPS or localhost.

Mesh-Detect MESH

A standalone scanner that uplinks labelled alerts through a wired Heltec V3 Meshtastic node on a channel you choose.

  • BLE + WiFi scanning
  • Meshtastic uplink
  • private-channel alerts

Plug USB-C into the XIAO Seeed ESP32-S3, the small scanner board, not the Heltec V3. This build flashes the XIAO; flash the Heltec V3 separately with Meshtastic firmware.

Public puts detections on the main LongFast channel (anyone nearby sees them). Private puts them on an encrypted acab channel only your nodes can read, and also needs the Heltec set up to match (see the mesh setup guide).

! Don't interrupt a flash. Flash on a stable internet connection and leave the board plugged in until it says done. Don't unplug it, power it off, or let your computer sleep mid-flash. The firmware streams as it writes, so a dropped connection or a pulled cable can leave the board half-written and unable to boot until you simply run the flasher again.
